About us

Chitkara University, located on the Chandigarh-Patiala National Highway in Rajpura, Punjab, is driven by a mission to shape future-ready professionals through high-quality, industry-focused education. Founded by visionary educators Dr. Ashok K. Chitkara and Dr. Madhu Chitkara, the university has quickly grown into one of the region's most respected institutions. Its lush green, modern campus provides a vibrant learning environment where academic rigor meets innovation, helping students discover their true potential and prepare for real-world challenges.

The university offers a diverse range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs spanning Engineering and Technology, Business Management, Pharmacy, Health Sciences, Art and Design, Media, and Law. What sets Chitkara apart is its strong focus on practical learning, state-of-the-art labs, and strong partnerships with global universities. Students have ample opportunities for international exposure, exchange programs, and collaborative learning that open doors to global career paths.

Beyond academics, Chitkara University places a massive emphasis on student success through its dedicated Career Advancement Services. With thousands of top-tier companies visiting the campus every year for recruitment, students receive exceptional placement support. Combined with expert faculty, hands-on training, and a supportive community, Chitkara provides a complete ecosystem designed to turn ambitious students into confident leaders and innovators.
